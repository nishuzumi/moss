Add the Neverland lending protocol adapter for Monad (Aave V3-based). Exposes supply, supplyNative, withdraw, withdrawNative, borrow, and repay Capabilities plus reserves, reserveData, accountData, and accountReserve Queries, with exhaustive typed Receipts that capture price and reward observations. Registers Neverland in the default MCP server modules.
